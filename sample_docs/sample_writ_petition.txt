WRIT PETITION UNDER ARTICLE 226 OF THE CONSTITUTION OF INDIA

MOST RESPECTFULLY SHOWETH:

1. That the petitioner is a permanent resident of Jodhpur and was serving diligently as Senior Teacher (Mathematics) at Government Senior Secondary School, Mandore, Jodhpur.

2. That on 14.03.2022, a memorandum of charges was issued under Rule 16 of the Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958.

3. That the Inquiry Officer concluded the inquiry ex-parte without providing copies of the documentary exhibits relied upon by the presenting officer, and submitted the inquiry report directly to Respondent No. 2.

4. That Respondent No. 2, acting as the Disciplinary Authority, straightaway passed the impugned order of dismissal from service dated 18.11.2022 without supplying a copy of the inquiry report to the petitioner or affording any opportunity of representation.

QUESTIONS OF LAW / ISSUES:
(A) Whether the non-supply of the Inquiry Officer's report to the delinquent employee before the disciplinary authority passes a final order of dismissal violates Article 311(2) of the Constitution and the principles of natural justice?
(B) Whether the impugned dismissal order dated 18.11.2022 is vitiated in light of the Constitution Bench decision in Managing Director, ECIL v. B. Karunakar (1993) 4 SCC 727?

PRAYER:
It is therefore respectfully prayed that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to:
(a) Issue a writ of certiorari quashing the impugned dismissal order dated 18.11.2022;
(b) Direct the respondents to reinstate the petitioner in service with all consequential benefits and back wages.
