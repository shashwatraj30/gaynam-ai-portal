## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ENGAGEMENT CHARTER

### 1.1 Context & Strategic Objective
The Indian judicial ecosystem faces unprecedented document volumes, multi-jurisdictional precedent discovery hurdles, and administrative drafting backlogs. **GYANAM AI** is an institutional-grade, sovereign legal intelligence system engineered specifically for National Law University, Jodhpur. 

Unlike generic commercial legal search engines or general-purpose generative AI tools, GYANAM AI combines:
1. **Deterministic Precedent Retrieval:** Hardware-accelerated search across **1,17,988 authentic Indian court decisions** (Supreme Court of India and all 25 High Courts) delivering sub-20ms query latency.
2. **Degraded & Handwritten Document OCR:** Specialized neural document processing capable of parsing vernacular police diaries, bail applications, and poor-quality scanned FIRs with statutory section extraction.
3. **15-Section Canonical Judgment Synthesis:** Grounded judicial drafting engine strictly governed by an Anti-Hallucination Precedent Guard (`GOOD_LAW`, `OVERRULED`, `DISTINGUISHED`).
4. **Data Sovereignty & Legal Auditability:** Full compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023**, air-gapped on-premise deployment capability, and **Section 65B (IEA)** SHA-256 cryptographic tamper-evident audit ledger.
