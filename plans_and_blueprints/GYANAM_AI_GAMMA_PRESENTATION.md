# Card 2: Executive Overview — The 5 Core Pillars
### **What Does the Portal Do?**
Gyanam AI replaces manual legal drafting and slow search engines with five dedicated, interconnected workstations:

1. **🏛️ Precedent Search & Real-Time Stream:** Query 1,17,988 genuine judicial records in milliseconds with automatic **Active vs. Overruled** precedent tagging.
2. **📑 Pleading Intake & OCR:** Scans physical court filings, cleans optical noise, and automatically strips private data (Aadhaar, PAN, phone) under the DPDP Act 2023.
3. **⚖️ 15-Section Judgment Studio:** Synthesizes complete, court-ready 15-chapter judicial decrees following official High Court rules, with full in-line editing and DOCX export.
4. **🔗 Precedent Lineage:** Visualizes the multi-decade evolution of landmark legal doctrines to ensure outdated or overruled precedents are never cited.
5. **🛡️ Audit & DPDP Governance:** A cryptographic, tamper-evident security ledger logging every access, review, and draft generation event for judicial transparency.
