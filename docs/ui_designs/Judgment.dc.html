Rangappa v. Sri Mohan

Decided 7 May 2010 · (2010) 11 SCC 441 · Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, Sections 138, 139

JudgmentHeadnoteRatioCited cases (1)Cited by
Curated paragraphs of the ratio · select one to quote it in a draftUse in draft
¶ 1Issue

Whether the presumption under Section 139 of the Negotiable Instruments Act extends to the existence of a legally enforceable debt or liability.

¶ 2Reasoning

The presumption mandated by Section 139 of the NI Act includes the existence of a legally enforceable debt or liability. Once the execution of the cheque is admitted or proved, the burden shifts to the accused to rebut the presumption by raising a probable defence on the standard of preponderance of probabilities.

¶ 3Holding

The view in Krishna Janardhan Bhat v. Dattatraya G. Hegde, (2008) 4 SCC 54, that the presumption does not extend to the existence of a legally enforceable debt, is not correct to that extent.
