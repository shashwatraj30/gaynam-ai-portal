Ratio decidendi · curated headnote

The presumption mandated by Section 139 of the NI Act includes the existence of a legally enforceable debt or liability. Once the execution of the cheque is admitted or proved, the burden shifts to the accused to rebut the presumption by raising a probable defence on the standard of preponderance of probabilities.
